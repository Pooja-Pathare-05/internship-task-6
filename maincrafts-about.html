SPEC.02 — About Us

Built by people who'd rather do fewer things well.

Maincrafts started as a two-person shop fixing other agencies' rushed code. That's still the standard we hold ourselves to.

SEC.01 — Our Story

Craft, not just code

Maincrafts Technology was founded on a simple idea: software should be built with the same care a craftsman gives a physical object — measured twice, joined properly, and made to hold up under real use.

Today we work with startups and growing businesses to design, build, and maintain the products their customers depend on. We keep teams small and senior, so nothing gets lost between the brief and the build.

We're not the fastest studio to say yes to everything. We're the one you call when it actually has to work.
